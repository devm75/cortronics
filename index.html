05 / Fine print

How to read this site

01

Prices & currency

All figures are approximate Indian rupees (₹). Only the Vol. 2 shopping list has prices quoted in the source documents; those are marked listed. Everything else is an indicative retail estimate based on common Indian component pricing.

02

Shared components

The core lab kit is reused across projects, so a project's estimate is the cost of the parts on that bench, not the cost of buying a whole new lab. Where a component is shared, its price appears in more than one project.

03

Flagship variance

Vol. 3 builds use custom 4-layer PCBs and single-board computers, so their totals swing widely with sourcing choices. The PCB line assumes ~₹1,500–₹3,000 for five fabricated boards. Treat these as order-of-magnitude budgets.

!

Mains safety

Projects touching 220V AC (Vol. 2 #3, Vol. 3 #2) require ≥6 mm creepage, a fuse + MOV on the AC line, an isolated supply such as the HLK-PM01, and a flame-retardant enclosure. Work de-energised and verify with a multimeter before touching anything.
